# StatusPass product brief

Founder: DINESH S

## Who it is for

International students in the United States on F-1, including incoming Master’s students, CPT interns, 12-month OPT graduates, and 24-month STEM OPT workers.

## What it does

- Pre-arrival: visa interview checklist, no-SSN bank catalog, eSIM, roommate profile
- CPT: full-time day counter (364-day hard alert)
- OPT: I-765 helper + 90-day cumulative unemployment clock
- STEM OPT: I-983 generator + E-Verify employer directory + 12/24-month eval timers
- Morning digest in the student’s `program_timezone` (default America/New_York)

## Freemium

- **Free forever:** CPT counter, OPT/STEM clocks, danger alerts
- **Paid:** I-765 autofill, I-983 generator, E-Verify employer search

## Voice

Anxiety-reducing. One next action. Never legal-advice tone.
